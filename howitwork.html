How LifeLink Works

LifeLink connects donors, recipients, hospitals, and blood banks through one simple platform, making the blood donation process faster, easier, and more reliable.

❤️
01

Create Your Profile

Register as a blood donor or recipient by providing your basic information, blood group, and location to become part of the LifeLink community.

🔍
02

Search Blood

Search for available blood donors or nearby blood banks using blood group and location filters during emergencies.

🚨
03

Send Emergency Request

Instantly send emergency blood requests so nearby donors and hospitals can respond as quickly as possible.

🚑
04

Receive Life-Saving Support

Once a donor or blood bank confirms availability, LifeLink connects everyone involved to help reduce delays and support timely blood delivery.
